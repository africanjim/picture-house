Picture House

Welcome! Three quick steps and your library will be ready. Picture House only reads from Plex. It never changes anything.

1 Sign in to Plex

A Plex window opens. Sign in, then come back to this tab.

Or paste a Plex token instead

In Plex on the web, open any movie, click ⋯ then Get Info then View XML, and copy the text after X-Plex-Token= in the address.

2 Find your Plex server

3 Choose a password

You'll type this once on each phone or computer that opens Picture House.
