Picture House

Your Plex library as a good-looking app for your phone and computer. Scan a disc in a shop and see straight away if you already own it.

⬇ Download Picture House

On your phone?

Picture House runs on a computer at home (ideally the one with Plex). Visit this page on that computer to download it.

Once it's running, open its address on your phone and tap Add to Home screen. It works like a normal app.

What it does

  • Posters & searchBrowse by title, genre, decade, director or recently added.
  • Barcode scannerCheck a DVD or Blu-ray in the shop before you buy it again.
  • Wish listOne tap adds a disc you don't have yet.
  • Works offlineNo signal? Your library is on your phone, and changes sync later.
  • Watched & episodesSee what you've seen and every TV episode.
  • PrivateEverything stays on your own computer. Read-only: it never changes Plex.

Setting up takes about two minutes

  1. Download and open Picture House.
  2. A page opens in your browser. Click Sign in with Plex.
  3. Choose a password, and you're in.
